# Reforbs Living — Review Response Playbook & Vault

> **Brand Voice:** Professional, empathetic, transparent, solution-focused, zero defensiveness.  
> **SLA:** Respond within 24 hours to all reviews.

---

## 1. 5-Star Review with No Text

**Template:**
```text
Thank you for the 5-star rating! We're glad to have you as part of our community at Reforbs Living. If you ever need anything, our on-site team is always just a message away. #LIVETHEVIBES
```

---

## 2. 5-Star Review with Detailed Praise (Food, Cleaning, Management)

**Template:**
```text
Hi [Resident Name], thank you so much for the thoughtful review! We're thrilled to hear you're enjoying the [mention specific amenity: e.g. daily home-style meals / daily housekeeping / peaceful work atmosphere] and that our team made your stay comfortable. We look forward to continuing to make Reforbs feel like home!
```

---

## 3. 4-Star Review with Constructive Note (e.g., Wi-Fi glitch, Noise)

**Template:**
```text
Hi [Resident Name], thank you for sharing your experience and for the 4-star rating! We're glad you're enjoying your stay overall. Regarding the [mention issue: e.g. Wi-Fi speed / evening noise], our operations team has already [mention action: e.g. upgraded our fiber router / coordinated quiet hours with residents] to ensure smooth living for everyone. Please feel free to ping the property manager anytime on WhatsApp if anything needs fine-tuning!
```

---

## 4. 3-Star Mixed Review

**Template:**
```text
Hi [Resident Name], thank you for the honest feedback. We're glad you appreciate our [mention positive aspect, e.g. location / room layout], but we clearly fell short in meeting your expectations regarding [mention issue]. We hold ourselves to high standards, and our operations lead would like to connect with you directly to address this. Please reach out to us at +91 74200 40241 or hello@reforbs.com so we can make this right.
```

---

## 5. 1-2 Star Real Resident Complaint (Maintenance / Deposit Refund)

**Template (Never argue publicly; take conversation offline immediately):**
```text
Hi [Resident Name], we are very sorry to hear about your experience. We take issues regarding [maintenance / deposit refunds] with utmost seriousness. Our policy guarantees transparency and prompt resolution. Please message our management team directly at +91 74200 40241 or email hello@reforbs.com with your room number so we can investigate your ticket and resolve this within 24 hours. Thank you for bringing this to our attention.
```

---

## 6. 1-Star Fake / Competitor / Non-Resident Review

### Action Plan:
1. **Flag to Google:**
   - In GBP Manager, click on the review > Click the three dots `⋮` > **Report review**.
   - Select: *"Off-topic"* or *"Conflict of interest"*.
2. **Public Reply while Waiting for Google Review Team:**
```text
Hello, we take all feedback seriously, but after checking our verified resident registry, we have no record of anyone by your name staying or booking with Reforbs Living. We strive to provide transparent, high-standard living for verified residents across Pune. If you are an actual resident or visitor who had an issue, please contact our direct team at hello@reforbs.com or +91 74200 40241 so we can assist you.
```
