## 1. Compliance & Legal Verification Requirements

1. **Written Consent Form:**
   - Under digital consumer protection regulations and Google structured data guidelines, resident quotes and photos featured on commercial websites require documented resident consent.
2. **Review Authenticity & Linkability:**
   - Screenshots of reviews cannot be crawled by search engines as structured reviews and do not pass SEO trust signals.
   - If these reviews were originally posted on Google Maps, they should be linked directly to their public Google Review permalink and formatted as crawlable, accessible HTML text.
