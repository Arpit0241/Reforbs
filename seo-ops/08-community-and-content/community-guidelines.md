### 6. What is the Real Difference Between a PG and Co-Living in Pune?
```text
In Pune, "PG" and "Co-Living" are often used interchangeably on portals, but the living experience is very different:

Traditional PG:
- Usually run by an individual landlord or unorganized operator.
- Strict curfews, restrictive visitor policies, shared common bathrooms in older buildings.
- Forced meal packages (whether you like the food or not).
- Long lock-ins (often 6 to 11 months) and deposit deduction disputes.

Curated Co-Living:
- Institutional management with dedicated on-site property managers.
- Zero moral policing; couple-friendly private rooms or 1RKs.
- Private kitchens or shared cooking access.
- Flexible contracts (Zero lock-in with 30-day notice).
- Transparent billing (sub-metered electricity at standard tariff).
```

### 8. Deposit Refund Disputes in Pune — How to Avoid Issues
```text
Deposit disputes are the #1 complaint in Pune rental groups. Here are 3 ground rules to protect yourself:
1. Always take date-stamped photos and a video walkthrough of your room, furniture, and bathroom on the exact day you check in.
2. Ensure your notice period policy is written: standard is 30 days before checkout.
3. Check the exit deduction clause: legitimate spaces specify a fixed minor cleaning/sanitization fee (e.g. ₹1,000) rather than arbitrarily withholding half your deposit.
```
