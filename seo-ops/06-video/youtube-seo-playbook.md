## 1. 60-90 Second Property Walkthrough Template

### Production & Filming Standards:
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 Landscape (YouTube) + 9:16 Portrait crop for YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels.
- **Resolution:** 4K 60fps or 1080p 60fps.
- **Lens Rule:** Normal focal length (24-35mm equivalent). **Strictly no extreme fish-eye or ultra-wide lenses** that distort room dimensions.
- **Lighting:** Natural bright daylight (film between 10:30 AM and 2:00 PM).
- **Stabilization:** Use a 3-axis gimbal or steady slow pan.

### Video Structure & Timeline:
- **0:00 – 0:05 (Hook & Street Proof):** Exterior building facade with visible road name / landmark (e.g. Sanjay Park Lane 2A street board or SLS College gate across the road).
- **0:05 – 0:25 (Bedroom & Workstation):** Walk into room; show bed mattress thickness, wardrobe space, air conditioner, and desk power sockets.
- **0:25 – 0:40 (Kitchen & Dining):** Show attached private kitchen (for Nirvana) or common floor dining area; highlight induction, fridge, and clean surfaces.
- **0:40 – 0:55 (Bathroom & Hygiene Proof):** Clear shot of washroom tiles, geyser, shower, and clean plumbing fixtures.
- **0:55 – 1:15 (Common Areas & Laundry):** Terrace view, washing machines, lounge, and biometric security entrance.
- **1:15 – 1:30 (Rent, Terms & CTA):** Text overlay with exact rent (₹17k/₹24k/₹29k), deposit (1 month), WhatsApp number (`+91 74200 40241`), and URL (`reforbs.com`).
