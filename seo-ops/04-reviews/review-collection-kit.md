# Reforbs Living — Review Collection Protocol & Kit

> **Strict Compliance Rule (Rule 2):**  
> - **Zero Review Gating:** Never ask residents "Did you like it?" before sending the review link. The exact same review link is shared with every resident regardless of sentiment.  
> - **Zero Compensation / Incentives:** No discounts, gifts, cashbacks, or lottery entries for leaving a review (violates Google GBP Policy).  
> - **Real Residents Only:** Requests go strictly to confirmed residents living at Reforbs residences.

---

## 1. Trigger Moments (When to Ask)

| Trigger Moment | Timing | Who Asks | Context & Mindset |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trigger 1: Settled In** | Day 14 after check-in | Community Manager (WhatsApp) | Resident has settled into their room routine, experienced daily housekeeping, Wi-Fi, and meals. |
| **Trigger 2: Issue Resolved** | 24 hours after a resolved maintenance ticket | Property Operations Lead | Resident's plumbing, AC, or Wi-Fi ticket was quickly resolved. |
| **Trigger 3: Move-Out / Exit** | During exit checkout / deposit inspection | Community Lead | Honest feedback on the complete stay and exit process. |

---

## 2. Ready-to-Send WhatsApp Templates

### Template 1: Day 14 "Settled In" (Warm, Zero-Pressure)
```text

Our on-site team is working hard to make sure your room, Wi-Fi, and daily housekeeping are running smoothly. 

If you have a quick 60 seconds today, could you share your honest feedback on our Google page? It helps other students and professionals finding a new home in Pune know what it's genuinely like to live here.

Drop your review here: [INSERT DIRECT GBP REVIEW URL]

No pressure at all! If anything in your room needs attention, just ping me here directly and we'll sort it immediately. 🙌
```

## 3. Physical QR Standee Brief (Front Desk / Common Lounges)

- **Physical Format:** 5" x 7" acrylic tabletop standee on the reception counter, dining hall, and rooftop terrace.
- **Header:** *"How's your stay at Reforbs?"*
- **Subheader:** *"Scan to share your honest experience on Google"*
- **Center:** High-contrast QR code generated directly from the property's Google Place ID review link.
- **Footer Text:** *"#LIVETHEVIBES • Reforbs Living"*
- **Rule:** Standee must not display any stars, rating suggestions, or incentive promises.

## 4. Direct Google Review URL Format by Property

Google allows direct review links using the Place ID format:
`https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=[PLACE_ID]`

Once each property's GBP profile is verified by Saad, extract the Place ID from the GBP dashboard (**"Ask for reviews"** button generates a clean shortlink `https://g.page/r/[CID]/review`).
