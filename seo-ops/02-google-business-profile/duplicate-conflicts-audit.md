## 2. Step-by-Step Duplicate Removal & Merging Protocol

If a duplicate or rogue pin is found:
1. Open Google Maps on desktop while logged in.
2. Search for the duplicate listing name.
3. Click **Suggest an edit** > **Close or remove**.
4. Select **Duplicate of another place**.
5. When prompted *"Select the duplicate place"*, search for and select the canonical claimed Reforbs Living listing.
6. Click **Submit**. Google typically resolves verified duplicate requests within 48 to 72 hours.
